We are incredibly happy and grateful to announce that ED Robotics has been awarded a grant of **SEK 50,000** from the **Syskonen Inger och Sixten Norheds Foundation**.

The news comes after we had already made the trip to the FIRST Robotics Competition in Orlando, and it still means a great deal to the club — the grant helps us cover the costs the trip involved and gives us a stronger footing to keep pushing forward, with new competitions and projects already on the way.

The foundation's support goes to students who don't always have the same opportunities to try advanced technology and engineering, and that's exactly the kind of opportunity ED Robotics wants to keep offering: letting curiosity and hard work take our members all the way to the competition field.

A big, warm thank you to the Syskonen Inger och Sixten Norheds Foundation for the trust and support. It makes a real difference for us, both for what has already happened and for what's coming next.
