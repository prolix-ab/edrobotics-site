It is with sadness that we have received the news of Fredrik Löfgren's passing.

Fredrik did so much. The context in which we got to meet him is one of his countless commitments to young people and their development, RoboCup Junior. It was our first robotics competition, an important milestone and a fantastic experience that laid the foundation for the club's future work.

![Fredrik Löfgren together with members of ED Robotics at a RoboCup Junior competition](/images/blog/till-minne-fredrik-lofgren.jpg)

We are immensely happy and grateful to have had the opportunity to meet Fredrik and to be part of what he stood for. As a club made up of young people, we have felt the effects of his commitment ourselves, and for many of us, our interest in robotics has changed our lives.

Despite Fredrik's passing, his visions and dedication will continue to live on and develop in other ways. He has left his mark on many, including us, and ED Robotics will continue working toward our vision of inspiring and challenging young people and giving them the opportunity to work with robotics and technology.

Thank you for everything, Fredrik. Rest in peace.
